# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome beginners, credit contributors and discuss evidence rather than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Keep issues and reviews focused on the project. Do not demand unpaid work or pressure contributors to share private saves. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the reason when practical.

Report a problem through GitHub's reporting tools. For a sensitive project security problem, use the private process in SECURITY.md. This project does not publish a personal contact address.
