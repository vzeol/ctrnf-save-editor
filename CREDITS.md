# Credits and thanks / Crédits et remerciements

This save editor exists because a much larger community made CTR Nitro-Fueled
playable and accessible on PC. Our thanks go to everyone below, to the upstream
contributors and to the players testing and documenting their work.

Cet éditeur a pu voir le jour grâce au travail de toute cette communauté autour
de CTR Nitro-Fueled sur PC. Merci à toutes les personnes citées ci-dessous, aux
contributeurs des projets d'origine et aux joueurs qui les testent et les documentent.

## OCTR and the projects behind it

The [OCTR Nitro-Fueled PC launcher](https://github.com/Ryu7w7/OCTR-NitroFueled-PC)
provided the visual reference for our GUI: its blue palette, header and navigation.
The following acknowledgements follow OCTR's own credits and project history.

| [T3CHNOLOG1C](https://github.com/T3CHNOLOG1C) | [SacredFire](https://github.com/T3CHNOLOG1C/SacredFire), the native CTR port |
